Product Definition and Enterprise Value Drivers

The moat is workflow, not voice conversation.

1. Time-Boxed Session Orchestration

Session structure:

Goal → Rubric → Reps → Timed Feedback → Next Drill

The agent actively manages pacing to finish within a defined time window.

This is structured coaching, not chat.

2. Context Grounding

All scenarios must anchor to:

Approved playbooks

Compliance rules

Internal knowledge

No improvisation outside approved sources.

3. Outcome-Linked Scorecards

Scorecards must connect to business metrics.

Enterprises demand measurable impact.

4. Tone and Delivery Signals

Allowed:

Pace

Fillers

Interruptions

Hesitations

Avoid:

Emotion labeling

Confidence inference

Tone signals should remain optional and user-controlled.

Regulatory and Legal Risk Summary
Biometric and Voice Data

Illinois and Texas explicitly include voiceprints as biometric identifiers.

Mitigation

No voiceprint storage

No speaker identification

Ephemeral session-bound metrics

Explicit workforce consent

Strict retention controls

Recording Consent

State-level all-party consent complexity.

Mitigation

Roleplay-only default

Configurable consent flows

Conservative compliance modes

Employment Discrimination Risk

EEOC warns about AI usage in employment decisions.

Mitigation

Prohibit sole-decision usage

Provide accommodation pathways

Avoid confidence-based labeling

FTC Risk

AI claims must be substantiated.

Mitigation

Evidence-backed marketing

Conservative biometric disclosures

Technical Feasibility and Risks

Mitigation:
